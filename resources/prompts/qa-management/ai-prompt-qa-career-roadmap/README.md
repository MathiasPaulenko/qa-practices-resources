# AI Prompt: QA Career Roadmap Planning — Companion

Companion files for the [AI Prompt: QA Career Roadmap Planning](https://qapractices.com/prompts/ai-prompt-qa-career-roadmap) resource.

## Files

| File | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `prompt-template.txt` | The full QA career roadmap prompt, ready to copy into an LLM. |
| `example-input.txt` | A filled-in profile: Mid QA moving toward Senior QA / SDET on the automation track. |
| `example-output.md` | A realistic output fragment: skill ratings, gap analysis, learning path, certification pick and 6-month milestones. |

## Usage

1. Copy `prompt-template.txt` into your LLM.
2. Replace the bracketed placeholders with your real profile, goals, weekly hours and budget — the more honest the ratings, the better the roadmap.
3. Compare the response shape with `example-output.md`; if a section is missing, ask for that numbered section again.
4. Treat certification and course suggestions as a ranked shortlist: pick what your market filters by, skip the rest.
5. Re-run every 6 months with updated ratings and compare against the previous plan.

## Requirements

- No tooling required: the prompt works in any chat-based LLM.
- Do not paste confidential employer information (salary details, internal org charts, unreleased plans) into the prompt.
