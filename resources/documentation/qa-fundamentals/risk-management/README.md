# Risk Management in Testing Companion

Companion for the QAPractices guide [Risk Management in Testing](https://qapractices.com/documentation/risk-management/).

## What's inside

- **`risk-register.csv`** — the worked example from the guide: three scored risks with owners, mitigations and status. Open it in any spreadsheet tool.
- **`risk-register-template.md`** — an empty markdown register ready to paste into your repo or wiki.

## How to use

1. Copy `risk-register.csv` into your project (or open it in Sheets/Excel).
2. Score each risk as Likelihood (1–5) × Impact (1–5). Bands: 15–25 Critical, 8–14 High, 4–7 Medium, 1–3 Low.
3. Assign an owner and mitigation to every Critical and High row.
4. Review the register every sprint — it is a living document, not a kickoff artifact.
